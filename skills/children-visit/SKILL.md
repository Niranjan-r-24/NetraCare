# Children's visit

Clinic guidance for parents and young patients under 16 visiting NetraCare Eye Clinic.
Taken from `doctors.md`, `procedures.md` (Children's eye check), and `clinic_policy_faq.md` (Children).

## Key Information

1. **Doctor & Speciality:**
   - All children under 16 are seen by our Paediatric eye specialist, **Dr. Vikram Menon** (Paediatric Ophthalmology).
   - Whatever the eye complaint (routine check, squint, sitting close to TV, school board difficulties), children under 16 see Dr. Vikram Menon.

2. **OPD Days & Timings:**
   - Dr. Vikram Menon holds OPD on: **Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday** (both morning AM and afternoon PM sessions).
   - Dr. Menon is not at the clinic on Wednesday and Friday.

3. **Parent / Guardian Attendance:**
   - A parent or guardian must come and stay with the child for the **whole visit**.

4. **What to Bring:**
   - The child's current glasses (if any).
   - Any school eye-check report or previous prescriptions.
   - A snack and a favourite toy to help keep the child relaxed.

5. **Visit Duration & Dilating Drops:**
   - Plan for about **1½ hours** at the clinic.
   - The check includes vision tested with pictures or letters to suit the child's age, a squint check, and a glasses power check with drops that relax focusing.
   - The eye drops take about **40 minutes** to work.
   - **After the drops:** The child's near vision is blurred and pupils stay large for up to a day. A cap or sunglasses help outdoors.

6. **Indicative Cost:**
   - **₹900**, which includes the full consultation.

## Steps

1. Explain that at NetraCare all children under 16 see Dr. Vikram Menon in Paediatric Ophthalmology.
2. Provide the essential details: OPD days (Mon, Tue, Thu, Sat), that a parent/guardian must stay, visit duration (~1½ hours), drops duration (up to a day), and indicative cost (₹900).
3. Offer to find an available slot with Dr. Vikram Menon in Paediatric.
4. Give the source as: `Skill: children-visit`.

## Rules

- Never diagnose or guess a child's eye condition.
- Never prescribe or advise medications or eye drops.
- Always cite: `Skill: children-visit`.
